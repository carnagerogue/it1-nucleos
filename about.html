About iT1 Nucleos

Empowering learners.
Transforming lives.

iT1 Nucleos was formed when iT1 Source acquired Nucleos Inc. — uniting iT1's enterprise scale with Nucleos's public-benefit mission to promote a more just society through education and training.

The acquisition

Two companies. One mission.

Nucleos started in 2017 as a public benefit corporation backed by the National Science Foundation, with a clear conviction: education changes outcomes — even (especially) for the people the system has counted out. After years of partnership, iT1 Source acquired Nucleos to scale that mission with the full weight of an enterprise technology partner behind it.

Today, iT1 Nucleos delivers Achieve DXP, hardware, services, and managed deployments as a single, accountable program — from initial procurement through every learner outcome.

Our manifesto

We believe that every person deserves a path forward. That education is the most reliable engine of dignity, mobility, and second chances. And that the technology serving the people the system has counted out should be held to the highest standard — not the lowest.

That belief is why Nucleos was founded as a public benefit company. It's why iT1 Source acquired us. And it's the standard we hold ourselves to every day.

What we stand for

Four principles, lived every day

Outcomes over outputs

We don't ship features. We ship completed courses, earned credentials, and successful reentries. The metric is impact.

Security as respect

Our learners and partners deserve a platform that takes their data, their dignity, and their context seriously — by design, not by exception.

Partnership over transaction

From iT1: relationships compound. We're not in this for the PO. We're in this for the program — and the people in it.

Evidence, always

Every claim we make is measurable. Every program is reported. Every funder, board, and oversight body deserves the receipts.

Our story

A decade of unlocking potential

2017

Nucleos founded

A public benefit corporation built around a simple thesis: education is the most reliable engine of second chances. Backed by the National Science Foundation.

2019

Achieve DXP launches

The first digital experience platform purpose-built for the security-sensitive realities of correctional learning.

2021

iT1 partnership begins

Joint deployments of Achieve DXP with iT1's tablet, networking, and managed services across multiple state DOCs.

2024

Coursera + Cisco integrations

Strategic content partnerships unlock industry-recognized credentials and pathways to real employment outcomes.

2026

iT1 Source acquires Nucleos

The partnership becomes a company. iT1 Nucleos is formed to scale the platform with the full weight of an enterprise tech partner.
