Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why we use them, and how to manage your preferences.

Effective Date: May 20, 2026
Last Updated: May 20, 2026
This Cookie Policy explains how iT1 Nucleos uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our public websites. For information about how we handle personal data more broadly, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to make them work more efficiently, and to provide information to the website owners. Similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, local storage, and tags serve comparable purposes; we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies."

How we categorize the cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for our website to function. They cannot be switched off in our system. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms. You can configure your browser to block these cookies, but parts of our website will not work.

Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this information to improve our content and user experience. You can opt out of analytics cookies using the cookie banner on our site or the Cookie preferences link in our footer.

Marketing cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites. They do not store directly personal information but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. You can opt out of marketing cookies using the cookie banner on our site or the Cookie preferences link in our footer.

Managing your preferences

Read our Privacy Policy

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the Cookie preferences link in our website footer, or by clearing your browser cookies (which will cause the consent banner to appear on your next visit).

Most browsers also let you control cookies through their settings. Visit your browser's help pages for instructions on how to delete or block cookies. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies will impair the functionality of our website.

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is currently no industry-standard interpretation of DNT signals. We do not respond to browser DNT signals at this time, but we do honor the choices you make through our cookie banner and the global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
