1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically when you use our services, and information from third parties.

Information you provide

  • Account & contact details: name, email address, mailing address, phone number, company or organization, job title.
  • Procurement & billing details: billing contacts, purchase-order information, and (where applicable) credit card or banking details processed through PCI-compliant third-party payment processors.
  • Account credentials: usernames, hashed passwords, and authentication metadata.
  • Communications: any information you share when contacting us, requesting a demo, or providing feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, content viewed, click events, session duration, and similar interaction telemetry.
  • Device & technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and language preferences.
  • Log data: standard server log information including request timestamps, referring URLs, and error codes.

Information from third parties

We may receive information from identity providers, content partners, payment processors, and analytics services where you have authorized those parties to share data with us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Achieve DXP platform and related services.
  • Process transactions, fulfill orders, and provide customer support.
  • Personalize the user experience and surface relevant content, programs, and credentials.
  • Send periodic communications regarding your account, platform updates, security notices, and (where you have opted in) marketing about products and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and protect the rights, safety, and property of iT1, our customers, and the public.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms.

3. When We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With your agency or organization. If you use Achieve DXP as part of an agency, district, or facility deployment, your account information and learning activity may be visible to your administrators.
  • With service providers. Cloud infrastructure partners (e.g., Microsoft Azure), payment processors, customer support tooling, and analytics vendors that process data on our behalf under written contracts.
  • With content partners. When you launch a third-party learning application from within Achieve DXP, limited identity and rostering information may be shared with that partner under our integration agreements.
  • For legal & safety reasons. To comply with subpoenas, court orders, or other legal process; to enforce our terms; or to protect the rights, safety, or property of iT1, our customers, or others.
  • In a business transaction. If iT1 is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice and obtain consent where required.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain authenticated sessions, analyze traffic, and (where you have consented) deliver relevant marketing. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in our website footer. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

5. Data Security

We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. These include:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2+ across all production endpoints.
  • Encryption at rest using AES-256.
  • FIPS 140-2/-3 validated cryptographic modules across the production stack.
  • FedRAMP-aligned hosting with Azure FedRAMP High inheritance.
  • Regular malware scanning, vulnerability management, and third-party penetration testing.
  • Role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for administrative access.
  • Audit logging with cryptographic integrity for every significant system action.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure; while we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Learner records and credential data may be retained for the duration required by the contracting agency, applicable funder requirements, and accreditation standards.

7. Your Rights

California residents (CCPA & CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and (if applicable) sell.
  • Request access to and a copy of your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law.
  • Limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information.
  • Be free from retaliation for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@iT1.com or 1-877-777-5995.

European Economic Area, UK & Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

8. Student Data (FERPA & COPPA)

When iT1 Nucleos acts as a "school official" or "service provider" under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), or state student data privacy laws, we process student personal information solely on behalf of the contracting school, district, or agency. We do not use student personal information for advertising, do not sell it, and do not retain it beyond the contracted term except where required by law.

9. International Data Transfers

Our production infrastructure is hosted in the United States. If you are accessing our services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice (such as via email or a banner on our website). Your continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
