What's inside

Six layers. One operating system for learning.

Every part of Achieve DXP is built to be deployed, audited, and reported on inside the most security-sensitive environments in the country.

01

Identity & access

SAML 2.0 federation with state identity providers (e.g., NCID). OAuth 2.0 flows. Role-based permissions and full audit trails.

02

Content library

Coursera, Cisco, GED, college, and vocational content — curated catalogs by program, funder, and population.

03

Analytics & reporting

Engagement and completion dashboards. Configurable reports for funders, accreditors, and oversight boards.

04

Security & compliance

FIPS-validated cryptography. TLS 1.2+ in transit. FedRAMP-aligned commercial cloud. Audit-ready by design.

05

Credentials & reentry

Industry-recognized credentials, portable digital records, and reentry pathways tied to in-demand vocations.

06

iT1 full-stack delivery

Tablets, charging carts, networking, lifecycle services — Achieve DXP delivered as a turnkey program by iT1.

Beyond release

The story doesn't end at release.
That's when it really starts.

Most learning platforms log a learner out at release. Achieve DXP logs them in — to a portable record, verified credentials, and a coalition of partners waiting on the other side.

  • Portable learner record — verified credentials and progress travel with the learner outside the facility
  • Direct connection to Honest Jobs — fair-chance hiring matches based on real, verifiable skills
  • Career pathways via GoEducate — industry-aligned next steps for every credential earned
  • Continued support — reentry and life-skills via First Step Alliance, FreshSTART, Vant4ge, and Untapped Solutions
