Why agencies switch

Three reasons DOCs leave the legacy tablet contract.

01

You own the data. Not the vendor.

Legacy corrections-tablet platforms treat the learner record as their asset. Achieve DXP treats it as yours. Open exports, OneRoster, agency-controlled IdP federation — your data stays with you when contracts change.

02

Audit-grade transparency

When a federal funder, a state auditor, or a federal monitor asks for the receipts, you produce them in minutes — not after a vendor support ticket. Every credential, every hour, every interaction logged, encrypted, and immutable.

03

Real outcomes. Real reentry.

Credentials that travel with the learner. Pre-built employer pipelines through Honest Jobs and First Step Alliance. Public-benefit accountability. The platform that measures itself by what happens AFTER release.

A word on the business model.

The dominant corrections-tablet incumbents generate revenue from commissions on calls, messages, and money transfers from incarcerated families — a model under increasing regulatory scrutiny. Achieve DXP is funded by the agency, not the learner's family. No commissions. No predatory fees. A platform purpose-built for the outcome, not the extraction.
