Why this matters

Three reasons districts switch to Achieve DXP.

01

One platform vs. three

You don't have to bolt a content vendor, a compliance dashboard, and a hardware contract onto your identity layer. Achieve DXP is identity, content, audit, and outcomes — in one accountable platform delivered by one team.

02

Audit-grade by default

Identity platforms log who signed in. Achieve DXP logs what every learner did inside every app — encrypted, immutable, and exportable in the formats your federal Title funders and accreditors actually require.

03

The credentials follow the learner

Identity tiles end at graduation. Achieve DXP delivers a portable, verifiable learner record that lives beyond enrollment — backed by employer and reentry coalitions ready on the other side.
